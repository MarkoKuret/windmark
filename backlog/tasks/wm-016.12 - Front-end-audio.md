## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
GameAudio (game/scripts/audio) plays the race from game/data/sound_map.json: boat sounds on every boat at their sidecar anchors, wind at the listener with the onboard pan, signals and race events through Hud.hud_event (penalty for the own boat only), countdown beeps (Full assist), ambience beds and one-shots, buoy and committee boat loops; buses own_boat, fleet, wind, race, voice, ambience, venue, ui made at run time with ducking (AudioBuses); Settings.apply_audio now applies the volumes (WM-016.25). Voices: 48 max, own boat first, 6 nearest other boats, wake only beyond 150 m. Default sound set synthesised by tools/soundgen (our own, Ogg Vorbis, about 770 kB); runtime synthesised fallback for wind, water and horn. audio.md updated first (map syntax: gate lists, trigger kinds, slots, synth, hails, course; new inputs). tests/audio_test.gd covers audio.md §11 1 to 6 plus buses, settings, pause ducking, wind pan, beds, real players and the sail scene on FakeSim and in a core race; added to run_ui_tests.sh. Not done: voice hails (no recordings), captions (§8), venue placed sources (no look files), Doppler option, rounding tick, sound_set setting, UI sounds.
