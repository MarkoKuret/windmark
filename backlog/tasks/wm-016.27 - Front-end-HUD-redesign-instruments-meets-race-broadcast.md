## Description

Owner (2026-09-25): the HUD is too generic; it should be opinionated, a hybrid between real deck instruments (B&G, Garmin: precise, high-contrast numbers) and the America's Cup / SailGP TV graphics (confident type, strong limited colour system, thin lines, data over the scene), not cartoony. First mock-ups over real frames (start, upwind, mark rounding, penalty, results) for the owner to react to, then build it into game/scripts/hud and update hud.md.

Owner feedback on the first mock-ups (2026-09-29): likes the direction, especially the overlays on the game itself (laylines, mark tags and so on). The instruments have too much going on, especially on the small screen (Steam Deck): add a player option to show more or less data (instrument density levels the player cycles with a key), and bring in a bit more of a real-instrument feel, just a bit.

Owner on the revised mock-ups (2026-09-29): level names Glance / Race / Data are OK; Glance's upwind cue stays VMG %; the glass backing is good but without the bold corner ticks. Still open: signature yellow for course and you, penalty turn in every camera, skipper or boat names on tags.
