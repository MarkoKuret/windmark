## Decision (2026-09-25)

- **Working name: Windmark.** The final name is decided later.
- **Candidates:** Windmark, pending an EU trademark search; **Sailmark** as the backup.
- Until then use handles and domains that do not lock us in: `windmarkgame` / `playwindmark`.
- Logo freedom, register re-check of 2026-10-05 and designer guardrails: `LOGO_TRADEMARK.md`.

## Rules for any name

- Short, one word if possible, easy to say in EN/FR/ES/IT/DE/SL/PT, searchable.
- Distinctive, not descriptive: "sailing simulator"-style names cannot be protected (EUTMR Art. 7(1)(b)(c)).
- Clear of event and class marks (America's Cup, SailGP, Vendée Globe, TP52, J/70, ILCA/Laser, Optimist, Nacra, 49er,
  Fastnet) and of existing sailing games and apps (Sailaway, Sailwind, eSail, AC Sailing, Virtual Regatta, Laylines).
- Works on a capsule with a descriptor: "Windmark: Tactical Sailing Racing".

## Owner checks before committing (all free)

1. **TMview** (https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/): `WINDMARK`, `WIND MARK`, `WINDMARK*`, `SAILMARK`; Nice classes
   9, 28, 41, 42; status registered or filed. Covers EUIPO, all EU national offices (incl. SIPO) and UKIPO. Look
   hard at Sweden and Germany.
2. **EUIPO eSearch plus** (https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/) with the similar/phonetic option.
3. **WIPO Global Brand Database** (https://branddb.wipo.int/) for Madrid filings.
4. **USPTO** (https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) again, including dead marks.
5. Company registers: AJPES (https://www.ajpes.si/), UK Companies House, a web search for "<name> GmbH / SAS / S.r.l.".
6. Store titles: Steam (incl. upcoming), itch.io, Epic, App Store, Google Play.
7. Domain prices: `windmarkgame.com`, `.gg`, `.game` premium pricing; RDAP lookup of the windmark.com holder
   (https://lookup.icann.org/). Buy nothing until the name is decided.
8. Social handles: https://namechk.com for X, Instagram, TikTok, Twitch, Reddit, Discord vanity, Bluesky.
9. Google the name with "sailing", "boat", "yacht", "game"; image search for similar logos.
10. One native speaker each for FR, ES, IT, DE, SL, PT says it aloud and reports slang meanings.
11. Confirm the name, capsule and tagline are clearly distinct from *Laylines* and *Tacking Duels*.
12. File the trademark once the name is final and there is traction (`BUSINESS.md`).

## Owner decision (2026-10-05)

`LOGO_TRADEMARK.md` (no WINDMARK or SAILMARK in any EU or UK register) that closes the name search. No lawyer,
no filing for now; the name and logo go ahead. Revisit only if the game takes off.
