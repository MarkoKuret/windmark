Race HUD: deck instruments, broadcast language

Mock-ups for WM-016.27, phase 1b. The overlays on the water stay as they were. The instruments get three density levels the player cycles with one key, and a little more of a real instrument: values sit on instrument pages (faint dark glass, a hairline top edge, corner ticks), every value has its label top left and its unit top right like a deck display, numbers are right-aligned in fixed fields, and a compact TWA dial and a heel bar are the analogue parts. Direction and rules: docs/design/hud_direction.md. The first version is kept for comparison: hud_mockups_v1.html.

Instrument density

Three levels. Density decides how many instrument values show; it never removes a contextual item (start, rules, penalty) or anything drawn on the water. It is separate from the assist preset (hud.md §14), which decides what help you get: an item shows only if both allow it. Defaults: Race on 16:9, Glance on the Steam Deck and other screens under 900 px tall. The last choice is remembered per device.

The level-cycle control

Keyboard
G cycles Glance, Race, Data, Glance. F10 still hides the whole HUD.
Gamepad and Steam Deck
View tap cycles (a hold is still the ranking). Deck template also puts it on R4.
On screen
Three small squares at the top right of the instrument page show the level (■□□ ■■□ ■■■). After a press, an "INSTRUMENTS · RACE" chip shows above the page for 1.5 s, as in the upwind frames.

Instrument pages

The same boat, same moment, at the three levels. Page widths: 486, 650 and 720 px at 1080p. Glass is ink from 14 % at the top to 44 % at the bottom, so it steadies the numbers without becoming a grey box. The TWA dial is bow-up: red and green sectors for close-hauled on port and starboard, yellow ticks at the target angle, the true wind as a solid pointer coming in from the rim; Data adds the apparent wind as a hollow pointer.

1. Pre-start

1 · GLANCE
2 · RACE
3 · DATA

Glance keeps the countdown and the one number to act on (burn), with the flag rule in one line; the start line, your course to it and the time to the line stay on the water. Race is the full start block of the first version plus the wind page. Data adds the third row of the page.

2. Upwind

1 · GLANCE
2 · RACE
3 · DATA

Glance: boat speed, the TWA dial with its target ticks, and VMG % as the one cue; the tack point, laylines and mark tag on the water are unchanged. Race adds heading, target, the wind page, the shift chip and the lap line. Data is close to the first version: VMG, AWA, the small row with a heel bar, gaps and a 6-line feed. The chip above the page is the 1.5 s notice after pressing the density key.

3. Mark rounding

1 · GLANCE
2 · RACE
3 · DATA

At the default level (Race) the rules line, mark tag, zone and live order are as before; only the instruments move onto the page. Glance cuts the live order to the boat ahead, you and the boat behind.

4. Penalty

1 · GLANCE
2 · RACE
3 · DATA

The penalty panel, the rules line and the turn on the water show at every level. In Glance the instruments shrink to boat speed and the TWA dial, which is what you need to sail the turn.

5. Steam Deck, 1280 x 800, Glance (default)

The Deck starts at Glance: race bug chip, one instrument page (boat speed, TWA dial, VMG %), the helm strip, a smaller radar, and the overlays on the water. Scale 0.82 of 1080p with text clamped to 11 px. Race and Data are one press away and use the same pages at the same scale.
