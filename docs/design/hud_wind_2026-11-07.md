# HUD: wind in the instrument cluster (2026-11-07)

The owner's note: the top right wind block feels out of place; the bottom left shows a lot of data.

## Decision

- The wind page (top right) goes. The wind lives bottom left with the boat instruments, where a sailor's mast
  display has it: TWA on the dial and the true wind speed at every level, TWD at Data.
- The shift chip moves into the heading cell of the page: the shift is what the compass tells you. In portrait it
  stays there too (a chip over the page would sit under the helm strip).
- The top right keeps race items only: the event feed, right under the top margin.
- Glance and Race show what a racer reads at a glance. Glance: speed, the true wind speed and the TWA dial (lean:
  it is the Deck's default; the owner, later the same day: the true wind speed is one of the most important
  parts, so it is on Glance too, 52 px beside the 112 px boat speed). Race: speed, heading, heel, true wind speed
  and the dial. VMG, SOG, AWA, COG, TWD and AWS are Data.
- TWD is not on the Race page: heading plus the dial gives it, and the shift chip says what matters about it.
- Nothing new is a ratio or a target (no VMG %, no polar %, no target speed, no HUD smoothing). The dial keeps the
  VMG angle notches and the green to red pointer.

## Pages (design px at 1920 x 1080; hud.md 16.3 has the sizes and the cells)

```
Glance 654 x 150 (was 486: a 168 px true wind cell between the boat speed and the dial; the width is what the
label and the unit need at the 11 px floor, UI scale 130 % on 1280 x 800)
+-------------------------+----------------+-------------------+
| BOAT SPEED           KN | TRUE WIND   KN | TWA               |
|                   7.4   |          11.6  |   (dial)   45° S  |
+-------------------------+----------------+-------------------+

Race 630 x 246
+-------------------------+----------------+-----------------+
| BOAT SPEED           KN | TRUE WIND   KN | TWA             |
|                   7.4   |          11.6  |                 |
|-------------------------+----------------|     (dial)      |
| HEADING                 | HEEL           |                 |
| [LIFT 6°]         305   | ==|==     12 S |          45° S  |
+-------------------------+----------------+-----------------+

Data 760 x 298 (it fits beside the compact radar in portrait)
+-------------------------+----------------+-------------+-----------------+
| BOAT SPEED           KN | TRUE WIND   KN | VMG     KN | TWA            |
|                   7.4   |          11.6  |       3.7  |                |
|-------------------------+----------------+------------|    (dial)      |
| HEADING                 | HEEL           | SOG     KN |                |
| [LIFT 6°]         305   | ==|==     12 S |       4.7  | AWA 31°S 45° S |
+-------------------------+----------------+------------+----------------+
| COG           306 | TWD            350 | AWS          15.7             |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

Going from Race to Data adds a column (VMG over SOG), the apparent wind pointer and value, and the small row; every
cell of the Race page keeps its place, so the eye finds speed, heading, heel and wind in the same spot at both.

## The rest of the screen

- Top left: the race bug as before. Top right: the feed only. Top centre: the alert lane as before.
- Portrait: the top row is the race bug alone, the alert lane under it; the page at the bottom carries the chip.
- Cost: one block fewer (the wind page); the chip is painted in the page, which is already built only when its
  inputs change (docs/PERFORMANCE.md, HUD cost pass).
