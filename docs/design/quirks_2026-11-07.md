# Quirks sweep, 2026-11-07 (WM-196)

The owner's report: "in solo free sail mode I often hear the penalty sound; that is just one of many weird quirks".
This is the sweep of every race event (sounds, toasts, banners, flags, feed, rumble, panels) for firing outside a
race or at the wrong time, with the cause and the status of each finding. The headless check that covers the mode
and timing rules is `game/tests/free_sail_test.gd` (run by `game/tests/run_ui_tests.sh`).

## 1. The penalty sound in free sail

**Status: fixed (the owner's ear to confirm).**

What plays is not the penalty signal. The race event path cannot fire without a race, at any layer:

- the core runs the race and the rules only once a race is created (`World::step`, `created_`); free sailing
  places the boat with `place_boat` and never creates one, so no rules event exists;
- the race HUD (`RaceHudValues.tick`) leaves `active` off while `sim.has_race()` is false and reads no events;
- the audio plays `race.penalty` only on the HUD's `penalty` event for the viewed boat.

The headless test runs three minutes of free sailing on the core, then two minutes steering about, the reset key,
a new world and a pause: no `hud_event`, no `race.*` or `voice.*` sound, no banner, feed line, flag, penalty
panel, ranking, results, name tag or finish card.

What the owner hears is `boat.sheet_load`, "the sheet takes the load" (`audio.md` §3): it fires whenever a sail's
trim settles (`motion` sheeting to idle), which with the auto trim is after every helm change, so a player at the
helm hears it every few seconds. The default sample was synthesised as a low-passed square wave sliding from
240 Hz to 150 Hz over 0.55 s: a tonal buzzer. The penalty signal is a low-passed square wave stepping from 523 Hz
to 392 Hz over 0.75 s. In the whole default set these were the only two one-shots with a tonal spectrum (spectral
flatness 0.003 and 0.008; every other boat one-shot is noise at 0.15 or more), so the sheet sound reads as the
penalty buzzer heard out of a race.

Fix: `tools/soundgen` makes `boat/sheet_load` as a rope creak of stick-slip noise grains that slow as the sheet
settles, over a soft block thump, with no sustained tone (flatness 0.116 now); the file is regenerated with
`python3 tools/soundgen/soundgen.py --only boat/sheet_load`. Nothing else in the set changed.

Open for the owner: whether the sheet sound should fire on the auto trim's own settling at all (now a soft creak
and thump), or only when the player trims by hand.

**Later the same day**: the owner noticed no change and liked the old sheet-load sound, so it is restored (file and
recipe). The remaining candidate is the ambience one-shot `amb/halyard_1`, a bell of three pure pings at 1.6 to
2 kHz every 20 to 60 s in every mode, close to `penalty_done` (two pure pings); it is now a noise-burst slap on an
alloy mast. Every one-shot's flatness and peak are in `sound_tonal_2026-11-07.md`; the gull is the next candidate.

## 3. Checked and clean

Read and, where a headless check was cheap, tested:

- **Session start**: the race HUD ticks once before the first step with the sim at tick 0; no event, banner or
  sound in the first second of a race (tested); the warning signal fires once after `free_sailing_s`, and not
  during a pause spanning its time (tested).
- **Events once per tick**: `RaceHudValues._events` is keyed on `sim.tick()` (`_events_tick`), so a HUD tick
  without a step (the reset key) reads no event twice; the audio's `tick` runs once per sim tick through the
  scene's tick work.
- **Free sail**: the race panels, penalty panel and turn ring, flags, rankings, results, course map, mark tag,
  tack point, VMG and shift chips, rules line, prompts and the feed are all gated on `race.active`; the name tags
  loop over the sim's boats and free sailing has one; `can_retire` and the results screen need `is_quick_race()`.
- **Pause**: the sim stands, so no trigger, contact or race event; the audio ducks and the rumble is off
  (`racing and not paused`); banners and the feed count sim ticks, so they hold through a pause.
- **Countdown**: `top_speed` and `distance_sailed` count only from the racing state; the countdown beeps need the
  sequence state and a valid previous race time (`NAN` after a restart).
- **Finish and results**: `race_over` needs a finished, DSQ, retired, ghost, closed or abandoned state of the own
  boat; the results screen opens on `results_due()` (the card's 5 s) and only in a quick race; the reset key is
  held while it is open.
- **Race again / Continue**: both build a fresh `GameSetup` (the session's with a new seed, the remembered one with
  the weather mode the venue still offers) and a fresh scene; `RaceHudValues.reset()` keeps only the names, sail
  numbers, skippers and the DSQ thresholds.
- **Menu backdrop**: its sail scene has `audio.audible` off and every player stopped (`MenuStage._quiet`), also
  after its own restarts; it is freed with the menu on a scene change.
