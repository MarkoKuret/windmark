from the linked pages and change daily; "not verified" means the figure or address could not be seen. Strategy is in

## 2. "Judge our umpire" pitch (rules creators)

Subject: **Is our umpire right? Three sailing incidents for you to judge**

> Hi NAME,
>
> I'm Marko, a sailor from Slovenia, and I'm building Windmark, a tactical sailing racing game for PC and Steam Deck.
> Its main feature is an automatic umpire: it judges the racing rules from the boats' positions, speeds and
> headings, calls each foul with the rule, and the boat must do a one-turn penalty within two minutes or she is
> disqualified.
>
> I've watched your VIDEO_THEY_MADE and you explain these situations better than anyone, so I'd like to ask you to
> judge my umpire. Here are three short clips (20 seconds each), each with the umpire's call:
>
> 1. INCIDENT_1 (rule X): LINK
> 2. INCIDENT_2 (rule Y): LINK
> 3. INCIDENT_3 (rule Z): LINK
>
> Is the umpire right? If it's wrong, I'd love to know why, and I'll fix it and say so publicly. If you'd like to use
> the clips in a video ("a game tries to umpire, let's see if it's right"), they're yours to use, with no strings
> attached. I'm not asking for a review: there is nothing to buy yet.
>
> Thanks for the videos,
> Marko
> Windmark, @playwindmark on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube

Pick three incidents the creator has explained before (rules 10, 18.2, 18.3 are the safest after the owner's check).
Upload the clips as unlisted YouTube videos or a shared folder; never attach large files.

### 3.2 The monthly quiz (what the club gets)

One image (1080 x 1350, the F12 freeze frame without HUD, cropped 4:5 for Facebook and Instagram feeds) and this
text block, in English and the club's language:

> **Who's wrong? NOVEMBER 2026**
>
> WIND_AND_SET-UP_IN_ONE_SENTENCE (e.g. "12 knots from the top of the picture. Kestrel is on starboard tack, Sirocco
> on port, both close-hauled.")
>
> **Who has to keep clear, and which rule?**
>
> *Answer (upside down, or in the next issue):* BOAT is wrong. Rule X: SHORT_RULE_TEXT. WHY_IN_TWO_SENTENCES.
> Penalty under the RRS: the Two-Turns Penalty (rule 44.2), unless the sailing instructions say otherwise.
>
> Who's wrong? by Windmark, a sailing racing game with an automatic umpire. More quizzes every week:
> @playwindmark on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

Rules for the club version: the answer quotes the RRS rule number and is checked by the owner against the current
RRS (2025-2028) and `../specs/rules.md`; no game marketing in the body; the game-specific "within 2 minutes" deadline
and the one-turn penalty are left out (they are Windmark's choices, `../specs/rules.md` §9.2 and §9.4, not the RRS
default); credit the club if a member suggested the incident.
