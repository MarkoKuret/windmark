# Venues

A venue is a real sailing area with its own character: the wind it usually gets, how that wind shifts and gusts,
the current, and the sea. The character lives in the venue file's `conditions` (`data_format.md` §6.7) and is told
to the player before a race by a short standard **briefing** on the quick race card (`menus.md` §1.3). The player
picks one of three weather modes (`race.md` §2.1): **Local** (the venue's own weather, full briefing), **Random** (a
wider surprise day, no briefing at all) or **Custom** (own wind, shift and gust picks; briefing without the Tip). The race
layer turns the conditions and the race seed into the per-race wind, current and sea settings (`race.md` §2.1), so
the core needs no new physics: every venue is the same wind (`wind.md`) and sea (`sea.md`) with other numbers.

Venue files are generated by `tools/venuegen` from a coastline source (§3) and a hand-owned conditions file
(WM-105); the generated grids and land are never hand-edited.

## 1. The venues

| id (working) | Place | Character | Tactical question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `practice_waters`, "Practice Waters" (renamed `example_bay`, WM-109) | open water and one island, hand-made | baseline: steady breeze, light current | learn the boat and the rules |
| `garda` | Lake Garda, north end (Riva, Torbole) | afternoon Ora thermal from the south, channelled between mountains, stronger along one shore; short chop; no current | which shore pays |
| `solent` | The Solent off Cowes | moderate SW breeze; strong tide that turns during the race, stronger mid-channel, weaker over the banks and inshore | play the current |
| `fremantle` | Gage Roads, Fremantle | the Fremantle Doctor sea breeze, strong, backing (turning left, toward the south) through the afternoon; ocean swell | stay on the side of the shift, surf the waves |
| `trieste` | Gulf of Trieste in a bora | gusty offshore wind off the Karst, big fast shifts, flat water near the shore, light current | head for the gusts, tack on the headers |

Note (Fremantle): the Doctor arrives from the WSW and swings through SW and SSW to S by evening, a counter-clockwise
turn (a **back**, a steady left shift), so its trend is negative.

## 3. Coastline and terrain sources

Only permissive sources (`CLAUDE.md`): every one used is listed in `docs/THIRD_PARTY.md` and credited in the game's
credits. OpenStreetMap (ODbL) is not used.

| Venue | Coastline (land polygons and drawn shore) | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Practice | our own shapes (today's island, quay and rock) | own |
| Garda | Provincia autonoma di Trento, "Laghi e specchi d'acqua" (lake outlines from the 2006-2008 LiDAR survey), for the Trentino north end where the racing is; the rest of the lake from the SRTM Water Body Data shoreline | CC-BY 4.0; public domain |
| Solent | EMODnet Bathymetry satellite-derived coastline (Mean High Water); the EMODnet DTM for the banks (Bramble Bank) that shape the current field and course exclusions | CC-BY 4.0 |
| Fremantle | Australian Coastline 50K 2024 (AIMS, NESP MaC 3.17; mean high water, from Sentinel-2) | CC-BY 4.0 |
| Trieste | EMODnet Bathymetry satellite-derived coastline (Mean High Water; Italy and Slovenia in one source) | CC-BY 4.0 |
| Terrain heights, all venues | NASADEM 1 arc-second (SRTM reprocessed, NASA) | public domain (US government work) |

Fallbacks if a source turns out too coarse or unavailable at download: SRTM Water Body Data (public domain) for any
shore; for the Solent, OS Open Zoomstack under the Open Government Licence v3.0 (attribution only; accepted by the
owner, 2026-10-06). `venuegen` records the source, its version and the download date in the venue file's
`source` note (`data_format.md` §6.1) and keeps the raw downloads out of the repo.

## 4. Performance budget

Venues must cost no more than `example_bay` does today: in the audit the venue is 3 to 5 draws and about 80k
triangles on a race camera, and removing it saves no measurable GPU time (`docs/PERFORMANCE.md`, "Venue" and
"Removing X saves"). Draw calls are the cost on the target machines, so the budget caps draws first. Each venue task
measures headless against `example_bay` (draw calls, frame time, core tick time) and must show no measurable
regression.

### 4.1 Physics and data (the core)

| Item | Cap | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Land polygons (`data_format.md` §6.5) | at most 4000 vertices in total, at most 1500 per polygon; simplified (Douglas-Peucker) with a 5 m tolerance inside `bounds`, coarser outside; clipped to `bounds` grown by 1 km | collision and AI land checks scale with nearby edges |
| Obstacles | at most 64 | |
| `wind_field` grid | at most 96 × 96 nodes, spacing at least 50 m | 8 sectors: at most 0.6 MB in memory, 75 KB per texture upload |
| `current_field`, `exposure` grids | at most 128 × 128 nodes each, spacing at least 50 m | |
| Venue file | at most 3 MB of JSON; loads in under 100 ms on the dev Mac | one load per race |

- **Lookups cost the same as today.** Wind, current and exposure use the one bilinear sampler of `wind.md` §8.1:
  constant cost per query whatever the grid size, so a big venue costs what `example_bay` costs. No lookup may loop
  over land, polygons or grid rows.
- **No new per-tick or per-frame work.** The conditions are resolved once when the race is created (`race.md`
  §2.1); the trend and the tide are the existing terms of `wind.md` §3 and §7 (a tide with a field already costs one
  cosine per current query, as `example_bay` would with `tide_scale > 0`; an implementation may cache `tide(t)` per
  tick like `global_from`). The briefing is computed in the menu when a row changes, never in a race.
- The land-factor texture on the water is uploaded only when the wind's sector pair changes, as today
  (`water_render.md` §4.3); the larger grid only makes that rare upload bigger.

### 4.2 Drawn land (the front end)

| Item | Cap |
| --- | --- |
| Materials | one shared land material for every land and scenery mesh of a venue (the existing land shader: vertex colour and value noise, no unique terrain textures), plus the existing water |
| Draw calls, main pass | at most 8 for the venue (terrain chunks, horizon ridge, scenery), at every tier |
| Draw calls, shadow pass | at most 2 (near terrain chunks only) on M and H; none on L |
| Terrain | at most 4 chunks around the racing area, each one mesh with 3 LODs: 5 m vertex spacing (LOD0, within 1.5 km of the camera), 20 m (LOD1, to 5 km), 80 m (LOD2, beyond) |
| Horizon ridge | far mountains and islands as one low mesh, at most 10k triangles, no shadows |
| Scenery (trees, buildings) | at most 2 MultiMesh draws, culled beyond 3 km |
| Triangles drawn | at most 120k on a race camera at tier M (today about 80k) |
| Drawn coastline | at most 20k vertices in total: 2 m tolerance inside `bounds`, 10 m to 5 km beyond, 50 m further out |
| Video memory | at most 32 MB per venue |
| Placed sounds (`audio.md` §7) | at most 8 sources |

The drawn land comes from the same coastline and terrain sources as the physics land but is simplified separately;
the shore drawn inside `bounds` must stay within 5 m of the physics land so boats never visibly hit water or pass
through rock.
